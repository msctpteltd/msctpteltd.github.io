Who we are

A dependable import partner rooted in Singapore.

MSCT Pte. Ltd. specialises in the import and distribution of foods, groceries and everyday consumer goods. From our base in Singapore, we connect quality producers with retailers and communities — handling sourcing, compliant cross-border logistics, storage and last-mile distribution.

We build long-term relationships with suppliers and partners, and we hold ourselves to a simple standard: the right goods, handled properly, delivered on time.

  • Trusted sourcingCarefully selected producers and suppliers.
  • Compliant logisticsCross-border import handled to standard.
  • Proper handlingCorrect storage for food-grade goods.
  • Reliable deliveryTimely supply to retail & business partners.
